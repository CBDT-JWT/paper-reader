Paper Reader 0.3.0 is a research workspace for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

- Native PDFKit reading, linked live Markdown notes, editable offline KaTeX equations and Mermaid diagrams.
- High-resolution PDF captures with page/coordinate source links, autosave and Markdown/PDF export.
- Native draggable folder tree, filename sorting, independent annotations, full-screen column-aware reading focus.
- Optional AI providers with streaming conversations and explicit note-edit approval.
- App icon, a drag-to-Applications DMG, a product website, and Sparkle update integration.

Download the DMG and copy Paper Reader to Applications. Keep backups of research files. See the README for capabilities, test coverage and remaining limitations. No private research papers are included in the app or website demo.
