Paper Reader 0.4.2 improves reading focus and its movement.

- One rectangular aperture contains the current line and its surrounding context. A continuous inward penumbra removes corner blocks and outward glow. Opaque edge overlap prevents bright seams at fractional pixel positions.
- Play/pause automatic reading directly beside the toolbar focus button. Adjust speed from 5 to 120 lines per minute and change softness, context and dimming in the adjacent settings popover.
- Manual arrows pause playback and advance through reading order. The PDF scrolls smoothly to keep the frame centred, including at page boundaries.
- Continue from the left-column bottom to the right-column top, reverse that transition, and advance between pages. Calibrated manual columns follow the same sequence. A brief aperture fade accompanies a column/page pan.
- Switching apps, changing papers, reaching the document end or disabling focus stops playback. System Reduce Motion settings are respected.

Validated with actual PDFKit and Core Animation pixels, full-screen transitions, speed changes during playback, forward/reverse column navigation and fractional moving edges. See docs/FOCUS_MOTION.md for coverage. Scanned PDFs require text extraction for automatic line progression.
